READING THE BASKET

Comparable, not representative.

Why these games?

They share creature collection, companion progression or closely related RPG/simulation audiences. This is a curated relevance basket, not the ten best-selling games in the genre.

What can it tell us?

Relative price positioning and an illustrative range of estimated units among selected peers. Using one estimator and platform improves consistency, but does not verify actual publisher revenue.

What can't it tell us?

Novabia's probability of success, expected unit sales or a market-wide median. This selection does not include a measured denominator of lower-selling, unsuccessful or less-visible releases; therefore no genre success-rate claim is made.

MOST RELEVANT · 10-GAME PRICE BASKET

WHAT WE WILL MEASURE

Validation means measure → compare → optimize → scale.

The round is designed to replace market assumptions with Novabia-specific evidence, not to reduce the opportunity to a yes/no question. External completion, retention, wishlist-conversion and purchase metrics have not yet been established. The categories below describe measurements we plan to collect—not performance claims.

PLAYER POTENTIAL
  • Vertical-slice completion
  • Repeat play
  • Companion attachment
  • Evolution-path curiosity
  • Technical friction
MARKET POTENTIAL
  • Steam visits & wishlists
  • Cost per qualified visitor
  • Cost per wishlist
  • Demo conversion
  • Geographic response
COMMERCIAL DIRECTION
  • Willingness to pay
  • Best-performing messages
  • Highest-response audiences
  • Regional pricing response
  • Supporter-edition interest
PRODUCTION POTENTIAL
  • Content-production velocity
  • Burn versus output
  • Team scalability
  • Support workload
  • Cost per milestone
