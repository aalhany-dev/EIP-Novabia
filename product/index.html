THE PRODUCT / 01

A game about companions.
A world built around ideas.

Novabia combines exploration, tactical battles, care systems, story, resource decisions and branching companion development. The current build is a playable development prototype; the next stage is to measure where the experience creates the strongest player response.

THE PLAYER BEHIND THE PRODUCT

Meet Ahmed — the companion-loving explorer.

Ahmed is a working player persona, not a validated demographic claim. His motivations connect Novabia's gameplay systems to the emotional opportunity: a companion worth remembering.

PRIMARY PERSONA AHMED · 22

He wants a game worth caring about.

Ahmed enjoys gameplay, story, world-building, and lots of pets and companions he can become emotional about. He wants to discover them, care for them, remember them and feel that his choices mattered.

THE PLAYER PROBLEM

Collection can become transactional

Once a creature is caught, the relationship can collapse into a checklist: Is it rare? Is it strong? Does it fit the team? What is its fixed evolution?

The emotional relationship risks becoming secondary to optimization.

NOVABIA'S RESPONSE

Turn collection into history

Care, story, branching development and meaningful choices aim to shift the player's feeling from “I collected this creature” toward:

“This is my companion. I helped shape what it became.”

THE VALUES INSIDE THE WORLD

Each region carries a concept worth discussing.

The ethical ideas are part of the fiction and gameplay context—not an afterthought added to the pitch.

01 / ELEMENTAL

Care for the environment

Nature is not background scenery. Environmental choices affect creatures, communities and the future they share.

02 / LANDSCAPE

War continues after battle

The consequences of conflict survive ceasefires through damaged places, displaced lives and burdens inherited by people who did not choose the war.

03 / KINETIC

Honor needs the bigger picture

A locally honorable action can unintentionally benefit an enemy or damage the wider community when consequences beyond the immediate group are ignored.

04 / CROSSROADS

Ignored suffering spreads

Neglecting the suffering of one society can deepen resentment, fuel extremism and generate future conflicts that eventually affect others.

05 / FANTASY

Do not generalize individuals

The actions of one companion or person should not become a judgment against an entire group.

06 / GEAR & PROGRESSION

Knowledge is strength

The most capable hero is not automatically the most armored. Books, music, medicine, education, skills and certificates are represented as meaningful forms of power.

THE LARGER MESSAGE

Care for the world. Understand consequences. Learn. Judge fairly. Care for humans and companions beside you.
