RISKS & RESPONSES

The risk is real. Our plan is to make it measurable.

Investors should evaluate these risks alongside the game, market assumptions, financial proposal and evidence-building roadmap. The approaches below are planned mitigations, not claims that the risks have been eliminated.

EXPOSUREPLANNED RESPONSEEVIDENCE TO TRACK
PC-market saturation & discoverability

Test audiences, store-page positioning, creative messages and creator channels before scaling paid campaigns.

Qualified visits, wishlist additions, attributed cost per wishlist and geographic response.

Differentiation & player attachment

Observe the care–progression–evolution loop with external players; refine moments that create attachment or friction.

Completion, replay patterns, companion-related feedback and evolution curiosity.

Founder concentration & key-person dependency

Recruit planned creative roles, document production processes, use shared version control and establish company-owned accounts/equipment.

Hiring progress, cross-role handoffs, issue resolution and delivery continuity.

Content scale, cost & schedule

Polish a bounded vertical slice and scale the strongest-proven companions, roads and narrative content through repeatable pipelines.

Production hours and costs per milestone, QA findings, burn rate and rework.

AI-assisted assets & commercial rights

Track asset origins, verify relevant permissions, maintain human creative review, and replace materials that cannot be cleared.

Asset register, documented licenses and release-readiness checks; see AI transparency.

Conversion, pricing & financing runway

Test positioning and willingness to pay; keep reserve capacity and prepare the next financing discussion around Month 12.

Wishlist funnel, cost signals, price research, remaining runway and next-round readiness.

Competitive references include major genre leaders and indie peers, but Novabia does not assume their reach or economics. A staged measurement program can reduce uncertainty; it cannot eliminate commercial or creative risk.
