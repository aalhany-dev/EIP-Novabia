BUILD VS. REPEAT VS. POLISH

What does the team do if we also use freelancers?

Novabia is internally led and produced. The $10K external-specialist allowance exists because a handful of high-skill, non-recurring finishing tasks are more economical to buy as defined deliverables than to maintain as full-time positions.
