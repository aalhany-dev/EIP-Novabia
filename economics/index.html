BUILD VS. REPEAT VS. POLISH

What does the team do if we also use freelancers?

Novabia is internally led and produced. The $10K external-specialist allowance exists because a handful of high-skill, non-recurring finishing tasks are more economical to buy as defined deliverables than to maintain as full-time positions.

WorkstreamOwnerWhat stays in-house / what gets commissioned
Core development & creative directionFounder / lead developerGame architecture and programming; core mechanics, progression and systems; tools and AI-assisted pipeline decisions; technical integration, design decisions, priorities and acceptance of every outsourced deliverable. Not outsourced.
Recurring content & operationsTwo graphic designers + writer + marketing/communityRepeatable character, companion, environment and routine UI assets; visual variants and day-to-day content iteration; story, dialogue and narrative production; community communications, campaign execution and audience-learning operations. Produced and improved continuously in-house.
Rare specialist finishing passesContractors, under founder sign-offTime-limited advanced effects, difficult rigs or signature animations, interface usability/accessibility improvements, audio mastering and selected technical finishing. Briefed per milestone with testable outputs, source files and clear IP/licensing terms. No standing outsourced development team.
