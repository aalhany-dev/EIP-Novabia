THE PRODUCT / 01

A playable journey.
A personal evolution.

Novabia: Legend Evolution combines province-specific story paths, companion care, tactical battles and cross-province progression. The website and design materials show a development build; features may change before launch.

CORE MECHANIC

Evolution is a decision, not just a level.

A Stage 3 evolution brings together two Stage 2 companions of the same species from different provincial branches. One remains the Heir. The other, the Tutor, transfers learned adaptation rather than physically merging.

THE AUDIENCE / 02

A focused first audience.
A wider world to earn.

Our current positioning hypothesis centers on adult players who like creature collection, JRPG aesthetics, discovery and team-building. This is a target profile from the project plan—not a claim of measured demand.

PRIMARY COMMERCIAL PERSONA18–34

English-speaking PC RPG players

Early geographic focus: North America, the UK and English-capable European players. Expansion markets and localization priorities should follow real player data.
