CREATIVE ACCOUNTABILITY

Tools accelerate production. They do not own the creative direction.

Novabia remains responsible for product direction, selection, integration and release decisions. Asset provenance, permissions and commercial-use rights will be reviewed and documented before release; the public tool list is not itself a rights-clearance audit. Company financing also creates a company-owned hardware and production-account base rather than relying indefinitely on personal equipment.

Product names are used for identification only. No sponsorship, official partnership or endorsement is implied.

CREATIVE PRODUCTION & AI TRANSPARENCY

AI assists the workflow. People direct the game.

Novabia combines conventional tools and AI assistance for concept exploration, visual iteration, programming, writing and production support. The founder remains accountable for creative decisions, game systems, narrative direction and final integration.

01 / HUMAN DIRECTION

Founder-led design

Companion relationships, world-building, evolution mechanics, gameplay balance and narrative choices follow the project's own creative direction. Outputs are selected, revised and integrated rather than treated as automatically final.

02 / PRODUCTION CAPACITY

A hybrid workflow

Existing AI-assisted and traditional software supports early resource-constrained development. The proposed funded studio adds two graphic designers, a writer and specialist support to increase creative consistency and production capacity.

03 / ASSET & RIGHTS REVIEW

Trace, check, clear or replace

Before commercial release, review each intended production asset's origin, relevant licenses, commercial-use conditions, modifications and third-party rights. Assets lacking adequate clearance will be revised or replaced. A completed project-wide audit is not being claimed at this stage.

04 / STOREFRONT COMPLIANCE

Disclose applicable AI use

Record applicable generative-AI uses and comply with the content disclosures and policies of each intended storefront before submission. Release-readiness checks will address relevant platform requirements, not assume approval in advance.

HOW WE EXPLAIN THIS TO INVESTORS

“Novabia is a founder-directed game developed with conventional and AI-assisted tools. AI helps accelerate parts of production; the product being financed is the game experience and original world. Human creative ownership, consistent quality, documented asset provenance and release compliance remain our responsibilities.”
